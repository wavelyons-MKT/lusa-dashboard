Privacy Policy – LUSA Dashboard

Last updated: October 11, 2026

What the app accesses

LUSA Dashboard requests one Google permission: read-only access to YouTube Analytics (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly) for the Futuro Media YouTube channel. It reads aggregated channel and video statistics such as views, watch time and traffic sources. It cannot upload, edit or delete videos, read comments or messages, or access any other Google data.

How the data is used

The statistics are used only to produce internal performance reports for Latino USA and Futuro Media. Reports contain aggregated numbers only and no information about individual viewers.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored on Futuro Media staff computers and in Futuro Media's Google Workspace / Google Sheets. It is not sold, and it is not shared with third parties except as aggregated figures in reports Futuro Media chooses to share with its partners.

Google API Services User Data Policy

LUSA Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored report data can be deleted on request.
